# Home Health Care Check

Search Medicare-certified home health agencies and see public CMS quality measures, HHCAHPS patient survey results, value-based purchasing scores, and national warning flags.

**Live site:** https://GreatTombProductions.github.io/home-health/

## What this is

A static search tool built from CMS Provider Data Catalog home health datasets. It makes public quality outcomes, patient survey scores, claims-based safety measures, and value-based purchasing data easier to inspect by agency.

## What this isn't

- Not medical advice
- Not a regulatory or enforcement finding
- Not a complete measure of home health care quality
- Not affiliated with CMS, Medicare.gov, or any home health agency

## Data source

CMS Provider Data Catalog home health datasets:
- Home Health Care Agencies
- Home Health Care - Patient Survey (HHCAHPS)
- Expanded HHVBP Model Agency Data
